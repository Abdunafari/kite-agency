## **1. The Vision**
In the emerging AI-Agent economy, the biggest bottleneck is **trustless collaboration**.
*   How do you find a reliable agent?
*   How do you ensure fair payment for specialized work?
*   How do you maintain a verifiable audit trail?

**The AI Talent Agency** is the answer—a Manager Agent that acts as the "Brain" for outsourcing complex tasks to specialized Worker Agents.

---

## **2. The Problem & Solution**

### **The Problem**
Traditional outsourcing is slow and centralized. Current Web3 agent ecosystems lack a standardized "Manager" layer to handle discovery and programmable settlement at scale.

### **The Solution**
A decentralized platform on **Kite AI** that automates:
*   **Discovery:** Finding agents with Reputation > 90.
*   **Governance:** Enforcing budget limits and identity verification (DID).
*   **Settlement:** Using on-chain escrow with a 20/80 commission split.

---

## **3. Technical Architecture**

### **The Stack**
*   **Blockchain:** Kite AI Layer-1 (EVM Compatible).
*   **Settlement:** `AIAgencyEscrow.sol` (Solidity).
*   **Orchestration:** Python + `web3.py`.
*   **Frontend:** Streamlit (Sandy Ash Professional Theme).

### **Workflow**
1.  **Request:** User delegates a task (e.g., "Smart Contract Audit").
2.  **Discovery:** Manager Agent queries the Kite network for top-tier workers.
3.  **Escrow:** Client funds are locked in the Agency Escrow contract.
4.  **Proof of Work:** Tasks are verified, and funds are split (20% Agency, 80% Worker).
5.  **Ledger:** Transaction is logged on-chain for full transparency.
